## 📌 Problem Statement & Context

Preparing for the **Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)** exam can feel overwhelming. Many candidates and #1MillionDevs learners struggle with:
- **Lack of Structure:** Not knowing how to break down the vast AZ-900 syllabus into manageable daily/weekly goals.
- **Time Constraints:** Balancing work, personal commitments, and study hours leading up to a fixed exam date.
- **Overlooking Weak Areas:** Spending too much time reviewing topics they already know instead of focusing on high-weight exam domains.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

The **AZ-900 Study Planner** transforms exam preparation from stressful guessing into a structured, personalized learning journey. By taking a learner's target exam date, weekly available study hours, and self-assessed confidence across AZ-900 domains, the app auto-generates a dynamic study schedule tailored specifically to their needs.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎯 **Dynamic Schedule Generation:** Input your exam date and weekly hours to generate a day-by-day study roadmap.
- ⚖️ **Domain-Weighted Allocation:** Balances study time according to official AZ-900 exam domain percentages.
- 🧠 **Weak Area Prioritization:** Allocates extra revision cycles to low-confidence topics.
- ✅ **Interactive Progress Checklists:** Track completed topics and milestones in real time.
- 🕒 **Countdown Tracker:** Displays days remaining until test day to keep learners accountable.
